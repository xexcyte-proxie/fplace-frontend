Using fPlaces

fPlaces helps you navigate venues and connect anonymously with other attendees. You must be old enough to use the app in your region and follow venue rules while on site.

Your account

Keep your login credentials secure. Choose a pseudo-name that does not reveal your real identity if you want to stay anonymous. You are responsible for content you post.

Community standards

Do not harass others, post illegal content, spam, or misuse Help channels for non-emergencies. We may remove content or suspend accounts that break these rules.

Navigation disclaimer

Map and AR guidance are aids only. Always watch your surroundings and follow venue staff and posted safety instructions.
